Meadow Memory is a gentle picture-pairs game for children aged 3 to 7, starring the 18 Meadow friends from the LittleLeap Studios stories.

Turn over two cards and find the friends who match. Every friend says their own name in their own voice, so even the youngest players can play before they can read.

GROWS WITH YOUR CHILD
• 14 levels, from "Very easy" to "All friends"
• Start with every card showing, then one, two and three hidden cards
• Then real memory with 6, 8, 12, 16 and 20 cards
• Poses levels: the same friend in different drawings, for sharp eyes
• The final challenge: all 18 friends, 36 cards
• Each level shows how hard it is with dots and a short label for grown-ups

MADE FOR LITTLE ONES
• Paw prints count every try, so children see their progress
• A happy celebration with stars after each level, then the next level starts by itself
• Friends take turns, so every friend comes round
• Big cards, simple taps, nothing to read
• Calm, warm and friendly: no timers, no losing, no pressure

SAFE AND CALM
• No ads
• No sign-in, no accounts, no in-app purchases
• No data collected
• Links for grown-ups sit behind a simple sum
