Meadow Memory: Privacy

LittleLeap Studios · last updated 22 November 2026

  • No ads.
  • No sign-in, no accounts, no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • Links that leave the game are behind a grown-ups check.

What the game does

Meadow Memory is a picture-pairs game for children aged 3 to 7. It runs entirely on your device. Pictures, voices and music are part of the game and are not downloaded from or sent to anyone while playing.

Information we collect

None. The game has no forms, no chat, no camera or microphone use, and no location access. It does not send anything about you or your child to us or to any other company.

Information kept on the device

The web version may keep a copy of the game's own files in the browser so that it can be played offline. The stars a child earns are shown only while the game is open and are not saved. If a future version keeps progress, it will stay only on the device and this page will say so.

Links for grown-ups

The grown-ups corner, opened by answering a simple sum, has links to the LittleLeap Studios channel on YouTube and to our music album Little Leap. These open outside the game, where YouTube's and the music services' own privacy policies apply.

Children's privacy

The game is made for young children and is designed to follow children's privacy rules, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU and Swiss data-protection laws, by not collecting personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
