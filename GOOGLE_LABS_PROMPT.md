# The Big Meadow Race: Google AI Studio prompt

Use this in Google AI Studio's **Build** mode (or Gemini Canvas) to rebuild or extend the game there.
First upload the pictures from `meadow-race/assets/`:
- Tomo: `tomo_walk_1…8`, `tomo_stand`, `tomo_cheer`, `tomo_happy`
- Zippy: `zippy_run_1…6`, `zippy_sleep`, `zippy_surprised`, `zippy_clap_a/b`
- Olly, Pip, Pop and Snail
- The meadow backgrounds `bg_S14` (Big Oak), `bg_S07`, `bg_S21`, `bg_S23`, `bg_S24`, `bg_S40`

---

```
Build a touch game for children aged 3 to 7, for phones and tablets in landscape. It's called "The Big Meadow Race".
It's from LittleLeap Studios and based on our YouTube episode where Tomo the tortoise races Zippy the hare.

USE ONLY THE UPLOADED PICTURES for characters and backgrounds. Never redraw, stretch or cut up a character.
Every frame shows one complete drawing. A drawing can move, mirror and be resized evenly.
Play the walk and run drawings in order as animation cycles.

CHARACTERS
- Tomo the Tortoise: calm, kind, quietly determined, loves strawberries. Red knitted scarf.
- Zippy the Hare: bouncy, boastful but good-hearted, easily distracted. Blue sneakers.
- Judge Olly the Owl: fair, a little dramatic, loves his golden whistle.
- Pip and Pop: cheerful squirrel twins with green and yellow leaf caps.
- Snail: tiny and polite, always "just passing through".

FOR THE AUDIENCE
- No reading needed. A warm narrator voice (browser speechSynthesis, slightly slow) says everything.
- One gesture: press and hold anywhere to make Tomo walk, and let go to stop. Big buttons only (at least 64 px).
- No losing, no timers, no villains. Wrong taps get a gentle response.
- Soft looping music under the narrator, cheerful sound effects, a sound on/off button.
- No ads, no links kids can reach, no data collection, no text input.

STORY (one background per place; Tomo walks in from the left and leaves on the right)
1. Big Oak start line. Tomo and Zippy are waiting. "Tap Judge Olly to start the race!" Olly glows until he's tapped.
   He blows his whistle, the narrator says "Ready, steady, go!" and Zippy runs off the screen.
2. Flower path, Mushroom Woods (Snail says "Good luck, Tomo!") and Giggle Stream. Floating strawberries
   (10 in total) are collected when Tomo walks into them. The counter goes up and the narrator counts aloud.
3. Sunflower Hill. Zippy is asleep on the path with floating "z" letters. "Shhh, tiptoe past." Tomo walks slower here.
4. Rainbow Bridge. A finish ribbon, with Olly, Pip and Pop clapping. Tomo breaks the ribbon, then confetti,
   a whistle and "Hooray! Tomo wins!" Zippy runs in late, looks surprised, then claps: "Well done, Tomo!"
   Everyone chants "Slow and steady wins the race!" twice.
5. Count the strawberries together, one by one, with big numbers. Then a big Play Again button.

If the child doesn't touch the screen for a few seconds, show a pulsing finger hint.
Grown-ups corner: a small TV button on the title screen. It opens a multiplication sum (like 7 × 8) on a number pad,
then a link to https://www.youtube.com/@Little-Leap-Studios.
```
